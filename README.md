# CoSupplyChain
CoSupplyChain

End-to-End Supply Chain & Financial Leakage Analytics

Executive Summary
As a Lead Data & BI Analyst, I conducted an end-to-end analytical investigation to resolve a critical business conflict for an FMCG & Logistics distribution network (DataCo Global Supply Chain). Despite an increase in gross revenue ($36.78M), net profit margins dropped sharply by 14%, leading to internal friction between the Logistics and Marketing departments.

Using Python (Pandas), SQL (MySQL), and Power BI (DAX), I analyzed over 180,000+ transaction records to pinpoint root causes, quantify financial leakage, and deliver actionable top-management recommendations.

Key Analytical Findings

1.Financial Leakage (-$3.88M Loss):
   Identified 33,784 negative-profit orders, resulting in a cumulative net financial leakage of -$3.88M.
   Uncontrolled promotional discounts (totaling $3.73M) combined with high logistics overhead caused high-volume SKUs to sell below cost.

2.Logistics SLA & Late Delivery Risk (54.83%):
   Over 54.83% of all orders suffered from delivery delays.
   First Class Shipping Mode exhibited a critical failure rate, with 95.32% of orders arriving late, driving up customer churn and Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC).

3.High-Risk Segment Analysis:**
   More than 50% of canceled orders and suspected fraud cases (4.1K out of 7.7K) originated directly from the Consumer segment.

Tech Stack & Methodology

Data Engineering & Cleaning (Python): Cleaned raw datasets, handled missing values, formatted datetime data, and preprocessed schema for relational modeling.
Database Management (MySQL): Ingested clean data and ran exploratory query structures to validate metrics.
Data Modeling & Visualization (Power BI): Developed complex DAX measures, configured dynamic visual interactions, and designed an executive dark-mode dashboard interface.

Strategic Recommendations for Executive Leadership
Re-evaluate Express Shipping Contracts: Audit and freeze failing First Class carrier contracts to eliminate penalty fees and SLA breach costs.

Implement Automated Margin Controls: Enforce mandatory price floors on top loss-making products to prevent automated discounting on negative-margin items.

Adjust Regional SLA Timelines: Extend scheduled delivery windows by +1 day for bottlenecked international hubs (e.g., Central Asia, Central Africa) to align customer expectations with realistic operational capacity.
